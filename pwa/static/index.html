    Contacts
    
      
      
      Adds people and rooms you hear. Only when the radio's contact list is nearly full does it remove the least recently heard contacts, to make room. Favourites (★) are never removed.

      
    Advanced
    
      
    
        
        
        
        
      
    
      
      Airtime factor spaces out your transmissions (1.0 is normal; higher is quieter). RX delay makes your radio wait before repeating weak packets. Leave both alone unless you know why.
